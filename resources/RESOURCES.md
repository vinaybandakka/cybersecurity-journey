## Reference material to keep open

- [NIST CSRC glossary](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary): settle any definition argument.
- [OWASP Cheat Sheet Series](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/): the "how do I do this securely" answer for developers.
- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/): shared language for attacker behaviour.
- [GTFOBins](https://gtfobins.github.io/): Unix binaries that can be abused.
- [CyberChef](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/): decode, decrypt and transform anything.
- [roadmap.sh Cyber Security](https://roadmap.sh/cyber-security): the bigger map of the field.

## Free books

- [Security Engineering, Ross Anderson](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/archive/rja14/book.html): the classic. The 3rd edition chapters are free to read online.

## Staying current (10 minutes a week)

- [tl;dr sec newsletter](https://tldrsec.com/): the best weekly AppSec and cloud security digest.
- [Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/): breach and cybercrime reporting.
- [Darknet Diaries](https://darknetdiaries.com/): stories of real incidents, good for commutes.
- [Risky Business](https://risky.biz/): weekly security news podcast.
- [CISA cybersecurity advisories](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories): primary sources for day 37's ATT&CK mapping.

## After the 60 days: certifications and paths

| Goal | Next step |
|---|---|
| Prove the fundamentals | [CompTIA Security+](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/) (your 60 days cover most of it) |
| Cloud security, using your DevOps strengths | [AWS Certified Security – Specialty](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-security-specialty/), then [Certified Kubernetes Security Specialist (CKS)](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-kubernetes-security-specialist/) |
| Application security | Finish the [PortSwigger learning path](https://portswigger.net/web-security/learning-paths), then the [Burp Suite Certified Practitioner](https://portswigger.net/web-security/certification) |
| SOC and blue team | [TryHackMe SOC Level 1 path](https://tryhackme.com/path/outline/soclevel1) and [Blue Team Labs Online](https://blueteamlabs.online/) |
| Offensive | [Hack The Box Academy](https://academy.hackthebox.com/) then OSCP later |
