# Day 57: Governance, risk and compliance (GRC)

**Phase 9: GRC, architecture and capstone** · Area: GRC · Planned: 2027-01-04 · Time: 45 min

## Outcome

By the end of today you can: Explain risk treatment, policies vs standards vs procedures, and what ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS and GDPR require.

## 1. Learn (15 min)

- [NIST Risk Management Framework](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/risk-management/about-rmf)
- [AICPA: SOC 2 overview](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2)
- [PCI Security Standards Council](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/)

## 2. Lab (25 min)

- [ ] Write a one-page access control policy and a matching standard (specific settings) and procedure (steps).
- [ ] Map three of your earlier labs to SOC 2 or ISO 27001 controls as audit evidence.
- [ ] List the four risk treatments (accept, mitigate, transfer, avoid) with an example each.

## 3. Explain it like an SME (5 min)

> Compliant is not the same as secure. Explain with an example.

Answer out loud first, then write a 3 to 5 sentence answer in your notes.
