# Day 56: Threat modeling

**Phase 8: Cloud, containers and DevSecOps** · Area: DevSecOps · Planned: 2027-01-03 · Time: 45 min

## Outcome

By the end of today you can: Run a STRIDE threat model on a data flow diagram and turn threats into backlog items.

## 1. Learn (15 min)

- [Threat Modeling Manifesto](https://www.threatmodelingmanifesto.org/)
- [OWASP Threat Dragon](https://owasp.org/www-project-threat-dragon/)
- [OWASP Threat Modeling Cheat Sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Threat_Modeling_Cheat_Sheet.html)

## 2. Lab (25 min)

- [ ] Draw a data flow diagram of a CI/CD pipeline deploying to Kubernetes, with trust boundaries.
- [ ] Apply STRIDE to each element and list at least 8 threats.
- [ ] Rank them and write mitigations as user stories.

## 3. Explain it like an SME (5 min)

> What are the four questions of threat modeling, and when in the SDLC should it happen?

Answer out loud first, then write a 3 to 5 sentence answer in your notes.
